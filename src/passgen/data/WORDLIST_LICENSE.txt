The bundled wordlist.txt retains all 7,776 EFF long-list source words in their
original order, without the numeric dice-roll column. It adds filtered entries
from EFF's short lists under the same CC BY 3.0 US terms:
https://www.eff.org/files/2016/09/08/eff_short_wordlist_1.txt
https://www.eff.org/files/2016/09/08/eff_short_wordlist_2_0.txt

It also adds filtered entries from SCOWL 2020.12.07 level 10. SCOWL's
copyright and permission notice is included in SCOWL_COPYRIGHT.txt.
https://sourceforge.net/projects/wordlist/files/SCOWL/2020.12.07/

At runtime passgen removes hyphens and deduplicates before uniform selection.
The source checksums, filters, counts, and output checksum are recorded in
WORDLIST_MANIFEST.json. These modifications are by passgen contributors.
No EFF or SCOWL endorsement is implied. The mixed-source data is separate
from passgen's MIT code license.
